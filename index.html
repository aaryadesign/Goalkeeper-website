    It asks before it acts.

    You stay in charge. Here’s exactly where the lines are.

    ✅On its own

    • Sorts your day
    • Writes your texts and emails
    • Reminds you, and checks back

    👆Waits for your tap

    • Sending anything
    • Joining a call, or calling
    • Paying, in the shop’s own app

    🚫Never

    • Pays or orders for you
    • Reads OTPs, chats or balances
    • Sells your data, or trains AI on it
